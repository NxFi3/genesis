# Genesis 4 research development log

Baseline: `b479449fe3ac1e8b9b1e9fc01d320e3396293294`. Remote heads inspected on 2026-10-21: no existing Genesis 4 branch. The complete threats and 3D development lines are retained; main is unchanged.

Audit priorities: full-grid BFS and tile-by-tile noise limit larger worlds; no age progression, households or inheritance exist; wildlife lacks livestock accounting; no controlled experiment runner or provenance-separated action analytics exists. Existing async cognition, authoritative transactions and SQLite archives are usable extension points. New systems must retain additive save defaults and the original regression suite. Milestone results and precise remaining scope are recorded below as implementation proceeds.

## Milestone 1 — scalable simulation foundation

Added coordinate-seeded chunk-addressable coherent terrain, correlated deposits and deterministic exact A* for larger maps. CLI and new-world API accept map dimensions up to 512 per side; 64/128/256 maps use the coherent generator, while existing/default small worlds retain their generator and saves. Five new tests exercise terrain chunk order, river gradients, geographical resources, 256-square survival/reload and barrier-respecting routes. Full suite: 161 discovered, 159 passed, two real-model skips (25.613 s). Renderer chunk streaming and large-world rendering measurements remain outstanding; generating terrain by chunks does not imply client streaming.

## Milestone 2 — households, lifecycle and inherited appearance

Added mutual adult partnership proposals/acceptance/refusal, household unions/separation and physically accessed jointly owned stores. Birth requires mutual intentions, real supplies, shelter/caregivers, stability and a population cap. Dependents cannot do adult work or enter independent inference; care consumes household food/water, education transmits skills and sourced knowledge, and adulthood enables new independent cognition. Configurable aging archives deceased identities/genealogy and leaves actual personal resources in collectable estates. Fictional seeded appearance inheritance is independent of personality and skills. Godot figures reflect inherited skin/hair/eyes, age, clothing/equipment; a Family tab shows portraits, relatives and historical records.

Twelve new Python scenarios cover mutuality, ownership, birth/care, an 1,800-tick childhood-to-adulthood/learning/application scenario, inheritance, death/estates, invalid kinship and exact SQLite continuation. Full suite: 173 discovered, 171 passed, two real-model skips (27.185 s). Existing Godot scene/presentation/isometric/threat checks pass; dedicated family asset/tree checks are added. Context construction now retains local climate/tick under tight budgets instead of dropping perception as the action catalogue expands. No live-model or new graphical interaction claim is made at this checkpoint.

## Milestone 3 — experiments, provenance and local analytics

Added validated JSON experiments, explicit baseline/ablation groups, matched seeds/repetitions, pacing and inference budgets, immutable output directories, failure manifests, source/runtime identity and exact offline physical-state fingerprints. Full CSV and JSONL streams are separate from bounded dashboard/chart windows. Model proposals, authoritative acceptance, completed effects (including linked production jobs), fallback actions and observer interventions are explicitly distinguished. Audit records do not enter agent cognition. Added provider-reported token usage with an explicit unknown flag, private-by-default request/response logging, per-agent counters, social/trade networks and per-seed/pairwise summary statistics without pseudoreplication.

Executed a three-seed communication comparison and a 12,000-tick/60-demographic-year run. The latter recorded three births and 16 natural founder deaths, leaving three adult descendants; this is an observed outcome, not a claim of stable replacement. A large-world profile exposed FIFO tie expansion in A*: preferring destination progress on equal-cost paths reduced measured expansions from 7,509,924 to 463,412 in the 256-square/1,000-tick benchmark. Before/after timings were 28.229 s / 4.888 s on this runtime; no target-hardware claim is inferred.

Full suite: 189 discovered, 187 passed, two real-model skips (28.113 s). Dedicated research scenarios prove real offline communication ablations, exact repeated physical results, source separation, invalid proposals, delayed completion, memory/shared-knowledge conditions, privacy, failures and dashboard HTTP integrity. The family suite now also validates a third generation. Real Chromium/Playwright rendered seven chart series from six completed trials, changed experimental groups and filtered an individual agent's history without JavaScript errors. Standalone Chromium CLI capture stalled in this environment; the subsequent controlled Playwright browser run succeeded. Godot integration checks are repeated before delivery. No real model has been available.
